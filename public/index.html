What you get

Plain English: what each part means.

No jargon. Here is what hosting, search, the lead dashboard and your Google reviews actually do for your business, and where we stop promising.

All plans

Hosting

What it is: Your website lives on a computer that is always on and connected to the internet. That is hosting. Without it, nobody can open your site.

What it means for you: We run it, keep it fast and secure, renew the security certificate (the lock in the browser bar), back it up and install updates. You never log into a server or deal with a tech company.

Your domain stays yours. You own your address, for example yourbusiness.com, so it is never held hostage.

Every site we build

Search (SEO)

What it is: SEO is how your site is set up so Google understands what you do and where. We build the foundation into every site: your trade and towns in the page titles and headings, fast loading on phones, clean mobile layout, a sitemap, and business details Google can read.

What it means for you: Your site is ready to show up when someone nearby searches for your trade. Growth and Pro add more pages (one per service or area), which gives Google more to match.

Straight talk: nobody can promise a number-one spot. Rankings depend on competition, reviews and time. We build it correctly and we never promise rankings.

All plans

The backend: your lead dashboard

What it is: The part of the site visitors never see. When someone fills out your quote form, it is saved to your own private dashboard and you get an email right away.

What it means for you: Every inquiry lands in one place with name, phone, email and what they asked for. Mark each one new, contacted, mockup sent, won or lost. Add notes. Tap to call or text. Nothing gets lost in a pile of emails, and you can see how many leads came in this week.

Growth and Pro also show visitor and call-tap tracking, so you can see how many people found you and tapped to call.

Growth and Pro

Your Google rating

What it is: When someone searches for your trade, Google shows a business listing with your stars, reviews, hours, phone and a map. That listing is your Google Business Profile.

What we do: Growth sets up your profile and keeps it accurate. We connect it to your site, add your review link so happy customers can leave a review in one tap, and show your real reviews on your site. Pro adds monthly profile updates, like new photos and posts.

Straight talk: reviews come from your customers. We make it easy to ask and easy to answer, but we never write, buy or remove reviews. That is against Google's rules and it hurts you in the end.

Pro

Google Ads

What it is: Paid ads at the very top of Google, shown when someone searches for your trade in your area. You pay Google only when someone clicks.

What it means for you: We set up and manage the campaign: the search terms, the ad wording, your service area and the budget limits. Your ad budget goes straight to Google, never through us, and results are never guaranteed.

Every plan

Edits and support

What it is: Changes to your site after it launches, like new photos, a price change, new hours or a new service.

What it means for you: Send us the change and we make it. Each plan includes a set number of edits per month (Hosting-only changes are $40 each). Pro gets priority replies and a monthly check-in on your leads and what is working.
