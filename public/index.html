What you get

Plain English: what each part means.

No jargon. Here is what hosting, search, the lead dashboard and your Google reviews actually do for your business, and where we stop promising.

All plans

Hosting

What it is: Your website lives on a computer that is always on and connected to the internet. That is hosting. Without it, nobody can open your site.

What it means for you: We run it, keep it fast and secure, renew the security certificate (the lock in the browser bar), back it up and install updates. You never log into a server or deal with a tech company.

Your domain stays yours. You own your address, for example yourbusiness.com, so it is never held hostage.

Every site we build

Search (SEO)

What it is: SEO is how your site is set up so Google understands what you do and where. We build the foundation into every site: your trade and towns in the page titles and headings, fast loading on phones, clean mobile layout, a sitemap, and business details Google can read.

What it means for you: Your site is ready to show up when someone nearby searches for your trade. Growth and Pro add more pages (one per service or area), which gives Google more to match.

Straight talk: nobody can promise a number-one spot. Rankings depend on competition, reviews and time. We build it correctly and we never promise rankings.

All plans

The backend: your lead dashboard

What it is: The part of the site visitors never see. When someone fills out your quote form, it is saved to your own private dashboard and you get an email right away.

What it means for you: Every inquiry lands in one place with name, phone, email and what they asked for. Mark each one new, contacted, mockup sent, won or lost. Add notes. Tap to call or text. Nothing gets lost in a pile of emails, and you can see how many leads came in this week.

Growth and Pro also show visitor and call-tap tracking, so you can see how many people found you and tapped to call.

Add-on · included in Pro

Your Google rating

What it is: When someone searches for your trade, Google shows a business listing with your stars, reviews, hours, phone and a map. That listing is your Google Business Profile.

What we do: For a one-time $250 we set up or clean up your profile, connect it to your site and add your review link so happy customers can leave a review in one tap. Google Profile Care ($49/mo with hosting, $99/mo on its own) keeps it active: weekly posts with your job photos, replies to every review within 48 hours, hours and info kept up to date, and a monthly report on calls and clicks. Pro includes both.

Straight talk: reviews come from your customers. We make it easy to ask and easy to answer, but we never write, buy or remove reviews. That is against Google's rules and it hurts you in the end.

Add-on · any plan

Google Ads

What it is: Paid ads at the very top of Google, shown when someone searches for your trade in your area. You pay Google only when someone clicks.

What it means for you: We set up and manage the campaign: the search terms, the ad wording, your service area and the budget limits. On Growth and Pro, every visit from an ad shows in your traffic panel. Your ad budget goes straight to Google, never through us, and results are never guaranteed.

Every plan

Edits and support

What it is: Changes to your site after it launches, like new photos, a price change, new hours or a new service.

What it means for you: Send us the change and we make it. Starter includes 2 small edits a month, Growth 4 and Pro 8. Extra edits, and all Hosting-plan edits, are $40 each. Bigger additions like a new page, a blog or an online store are one-time add-ons.

Questions

Before you ask for a mockup.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. We design a mockup of your homepage at no cost and with no obligation. You only pay if you decide to move forward.

What happens after I send my request?

We follow up personally, usually within one business day, to learn a little more about your business and get your mockup started.

How much does a website cost?

Plans run from $49 a month for hosting-only (plus a $399 one-time move-over) to $199 a month for Pro. Starter is $75 a month with a $750 setup, Growth is $125 a month with a $1,000 setup, and Pro is $199 a month with a $1,500 setup. The mockup is free.

What kinds of businesses do you build for?

Trade and home-service businesses: plumbers, builders and general contractors, landscapers, HVAC, electricians, roofers, excavation and septic, cleaners and metal-building companies.

Where are you located and who do you work with?

Real Easy Sites is based in Maine and builds websites for trade businesses anywhere in the U.S. Everything is handled online, so your location does not matter.

I already have a website. Can you rebuild it?

Yes. If you have a site, we can rebuild it. If you do not have one, we start fresh.

Who owns my domain name?

You do. You buy and own your domain, so it is always yours.

What does the monthly price cover?

Every plan covers hosting, security, backups, your quote form with email alerts and your lead dashboard. Starter adds 2 small edits a month, Growth adds 4 plus traffic tracking, and Pro adds 8 priority edits, Google Business Profile setup and Google Profile Care, and a monthly check-in. On the other plans, Google Business Profile setup ($250 one-time) and Google Profile Care ($49/mo with hosting) are add-ons. Google Ads management, blogs and online stores are add-ons on any plan that offers them.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can move up to a bigger plan any time.
